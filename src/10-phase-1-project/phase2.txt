11. Component Organization

Instead of putting everything into one App.jsx, we separate components into folders.

A professional structure can look like:

src/
├── components/
│   ├── Header/
│   │   └── Header.jsx
│   │
│   ├── StudentCard/
│   │   └── StudentCard.jsx
│   │
│   └── Footer/
│       └── Footer.jsx
│
├── pages/
│   └── Home.jsx
│
├── App.jsx
└── main.jsx

This makes a large application easier to maintain.
